The Workflow (How it happens)
Someone messages you: "Hey, what time are you free?"

Twilio catches this and "pings" your ngrok URL.

FastAPI receives the ping, takes the text, and sends it to ai_agent.py.

AI Agent checks your database history, thinks of a reply, and saves it.

FastAPI sends the reply back to Twilio.

Twilio delivers the message to the person's WhatsApp inbox.
